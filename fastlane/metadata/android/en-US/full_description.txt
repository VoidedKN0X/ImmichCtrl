<p>A native Android app for self-hosted Immich photo servers (2.x and 3.x).</p>
<ul>
<li>Fast timeline grouped by day or month, with your phone's photos mixed in</li>
<li>Automatic backup with Wi-Fi-only and charging-only options, album sync and free up space</li>
<li>Memories, slideshows and live updates from other devices</li>
<li>Viewer with editing, text from photos, faces, stacks and date/location changes</li>
<li>Albums, shared albums, offline albums and duplicate review</li>
<li>People, places, map, tags, folders, archive, trash and locked folder</li>
<li>Smart search with filters</li>
<li>Shared links and partner sharing</li>
<li>App lock, read-only mode, location removal when sharing, Show maps switch</li>
<li>Home screen widgets and a Quick Settings backup tile</li>
<li>Home and public address: you choose which one the app uses; it never switches by itself</li>
<li>Admin tools: users, jobs, storage and server alerts</li>
</ul>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
